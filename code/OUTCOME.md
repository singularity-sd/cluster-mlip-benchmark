# Outcome of the preregistered confirmation (3 November 2026)

Protocol: `PROTOCOL.md`, tag `prereg-idea10`, fixed before comparing any QCD energy with a model. The confirmation was
run only once, on the 28 elements never used before: 1,458 groups and 13,102 isomers.

## Results

| | MACE-MP-0 (primary) | MACE-OMAT-0 (secondary) | random |
|---|---|---|---|
| **right minimum** | **33.3%** (95% CI 28.5–38.0%) | **41.8%** | 12.2% |
| mean Spearman | 0.31 | 0.47 | — |
| error on relative energies (isomers within 0.5 eV) | 17.2 meV/atom | 10.0 meV/atom | — |

By size band (MP-0 / OMAT-0):

| atoms | right minimum | error (meV/atom) |
|---|---|---|
| 3–10 | 36.3% / 47.6% | 39.5 / 24.0 |
| 11–25 | 26.2% / 33.4% | 23.5 / 12.9 |
| 26–55 | 36.1% / 44.4% | 8.3 / 4.9 |

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (MP-0 below 60%): confirmed.** 33.3%, interval 28.5–38.0%.
- **H2 (OMAT-0 does not beat MP-0 by more than 15 points): confirmed.** Difference +8.4 points (95% CI 4.8–11.9,
  bootstrap over elements): a real but small improvement.
- **H3 (larger error for small clusters): partly confirmed.**
  - Yes for the error per atom: 39.5 against 8.3 meV/atom with MP-0.
  - No for the right minimum: 36.3% against 36.1%. The worst band is the middle one, 11–25 atoms.

## Exploratory analyses (not preregistered)
- **How many of the model's candidates to re-check in DFT.** The minimum is among the first 1/2/3/5 in:
  - 33/50/63/80% of the groups with MP-0;
  - 42/61/73/87% with OMAT-0.
- **At least one of the two models** finds the minimum in 50.6% of the groups.
- **Worst elements:**
  - with MP-0: Ga, Rb, Hg, Hf, Al;
  - with OMAT-0: Hg, Rb, Ga, Pd, Zn.
  - Pt and Au, important for catalysis, are at 21% and 29% with MP-0.
- **Development (27 elements) had given 32.8%** with MP-0, almost identical to the confirmation.
- **Magnetism does not explain the errors** (checked on the development elements).
